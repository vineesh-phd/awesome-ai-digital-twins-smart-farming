## Formatting Choice

The assignment names the ACM small format, so the classroom version uses `acmsmall,screen,nonacm`. ACM's own sample explains that an actual journal review submission may instead require the `manuscript` option. This classroom package is not represented as accepted, published, or submitted to ACM. Sample DOI, rights, volume, and acceptance-date placeholders are not carried into the paper. No custom page margins or body fonts are imposed.
