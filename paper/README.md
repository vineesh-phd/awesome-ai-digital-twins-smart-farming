## Scope

- Agricultural digital-twin architectures and operational feedback.
- Root-zone water balance, Richards flow, hydraulic closure, and crop coupling.
- Parameter calibration, observation operators, and state assimilation.
- PINN loss formulation, inverse modelling, and documented failure modes.
- Constrained irrigation decisions and outcome-based evaluation.

The paper contains 20 references and 12 numbered equations. It is a narrative review, not a systematic review or an original field experiment. The 200 m3 example is illustrative. The Word text is editable; display equations are rendered mathematics, not native editable Word equations.
